Case study · Self-initiated concept

MORROW & CO. — how the concept was approached

A fictional premium café created to show how I plan, design and build a business website. There is no client, no real business behind it and no performance data — it is a demonstration of process and craft.

01 / OBJECTIVE

Show that I can turn a business idea into a complete, credible website: clear story, easy-to-scan menu, simple navigation and a clear call to action.

02 / DESIGN DIRECTION

Warm espresso, cream and caramel tones with an editorial serif for headings and a clean sans-serif for reading. Original vector illustrations replace stock photos, so the site is lightweight and never shows a broken image.

03 / MAIN FEATURES
  • Full-width hero with menu and explore buttons
  • Filterable menu with clearly labelled demo prices
  • Featured products, gallery and experience story
  • Concept location and a validated demo reservation form
04 / RESPONSIVE BEHAVIOR

Designed for mobile, tablet and desktop: layouts reflow from multi-column to single column, navigation collapses into an accessible menu, and tap targets stay comfortable on small screens.

05 / TOOLS & TECHNOLOGIES

Semantic HTML5, hand-written CSS (custom properties, grid, flexbox), vanilla JavaScript and inline SVG. No frameworks, paid APIs or backend. Developed with AI-assisted tools.

06 / HONEST SCOPE

The reservation form validates input in the browser only. Nothing is sent or booked unless a backend is connected. All prices are illustrative concept-demo prices.
